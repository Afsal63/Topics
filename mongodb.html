⚡ Interview Tips
01
Write aggregation pipelines on the spot. Practice building $match + $group + $lookup combos from scratch. Interviewers love asking you to solve analytics problems with the aggregation framework — having the stage order memorized (match early, unwind before group, lookup after match) wins points.
02
Always discuss trade-offs for schema design questions. Never say "embed" or "reference" without explaining why. Mention document size limits, query patterns, write frequency, and the ESR rule. Show that you think about reads vs writes holistically.
03
Ground answers in real project experience. Mention your ERP system (erp-next.vercel.app), Dialar (WebRTC), or Freston Analytics work. "At Freston Analytics, we used compound indexes with the ESR rule to optimize dashboard queries" is far stronger than a textbook answer.
04
Know when NOT to use transactions. MongoDB single-document operations are always atomic — a common gotcha. Saying "I avoid multi-document transactions unless necessary due to performance overhead" signals maturity.
05
Explain explain(). Interviewers love asking how you debug slow queries. Walk them through COLLSCAN vs IXSCAN, totalDocsExamined vs nReturned, and how you'd add or restructure an index to fix it.
