⚡ Interview Tips
01
Always clarify requirements first (5 min). Ask: scale (users/DAU), read vs write ratio, consistency vs availability, geography, latency SLA. Interviewers want to see you don't jump to solutions before understanding the problem.
02
Do back-of-envelope math before choosing tech. "1M DAU, 10 requests/day = ~116 RPS. Peak 3x = 350 RPS. A single PostgreSQL node handles 5k QPS easily" — this shows engineering judgment, not just pattern matching.
03
Structure every design: API → Data Model → Architecture → Scale. Start simple (single server), then layer in complexity only when you explain why. Interviewers penalize over-engineered solutions that ignore simpler approaches.
04
Always discuss trade-offs, not just solutions. Don't just say "use Kafka." Say "Kafka gives us durability and replay, but adds operational complexity. For this scale, SQS might be simpler — here's why I'd choose Kafka…"
05
Identify bottlenecks proactively. After drawing the architecture, say "The bottleneck here is the database write path. Here's how I'd address it with read replicas / sharding / CQRS."
06
Know the numbers. SSD: 0.1ms read. Network roundtrip: 1–10ms. RAM: nanoseconds. HDD: 10ms seek. These let you reason about whether caching is necessary, what latency is achievable, and justify your design decisions.
